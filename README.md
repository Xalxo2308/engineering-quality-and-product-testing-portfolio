# Engineering Quality and Product Testing Portfolio

An engineering portfolio showcasing quality planning and analysis, consumer electronics product testing concepts, electronics inspection, defect documentation and systematic troubleshooting.

This portfolio demonstrates the application of engineering and quality-management concepts to a hypothetical consumer electronics product scenario, alongside documentation of quality-analysis methodologies.

---

## Portfolio Overview

This repository focuses on quality planning, product-testing methodologies, electronics inspection and engineering analysis.

The central illustrative case study is a Smart Wi-Fi Video Doorbell experiencing hypothetical operational issues, including unexpected restarts or Wi-Fi connectivity interruptions.

The case study is used to demonstrate how engineering quality tools can structure analysis, identify potential contributing factors, prioritise investigation and document engineering considerations.

## Case Study Disclaimer

The Smart Wi-Fi Video Doorbell scenario presented in this portfolio is hypothetical and intended for educational and analytical demonstration.

The portfolio does not claim that physical product testing was performed, that actual defects were identified, or that any root cause was verified.

Analysis and documentation are presented as illustrative applications of engineering and quality-management methodologies.
